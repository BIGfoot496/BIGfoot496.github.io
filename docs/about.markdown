Hey!

My name is Elijah, and this is (what might eventually become) my blog/diary/writing practice thingamajig! This whole thing is still under construction, and I've honestly no idea if it'll ever be built, or I'll abandon this like many other projects before and go do something else.

If you know me online, you probably know me as BIGfoot. If you know me offline, please tell me how the heck you managed to find this the next time we meet. If you know me both online and offline, 洋，歡迎你參觀，但我估計你會覺得無聊。

I love anything to do with languages: linguistics, language learning, conlanging. Love reading, especially sci-fi and fantasy, also scientific papers (even better if they have nothing to do with my actual work). Love cooking (and eating!) foods from different cultures and time periods. Love solving and creating puzzles. So this is likely the things you might find me writing about here.

Currently most, if not all, posts come from my attempt to read "a paper a day". Topics vary wildly (although a huge proportion are related to my current PhD studies, so there'll be a LOT of condensed matter physics), and I do sometimes stretch the definition of "paper". I honestly don't know why I'm doing this. Some of it is my giant "to be read" pile just for papers, some of it is the desire to have a place I can quickly look up the papers I've already read, some of it is just my masochistic tendencies flaring up XD
