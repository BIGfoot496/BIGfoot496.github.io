## A paper a day: day 23
Today's [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.3249) took me more than a week to read, and to be completely honest, I still have not finished certain parts, mainly in the theory section. It is a survey of the whole spin Hall effect field circa ten years ago, written by people who played a big role in creating it. As such, it is both way too big to grasp quickly and way too small to cover everything in any sort of detail. It was very painful to read at times: I'd very much rather read several papers, each covering its own tiny topic, than one that jumps all over the place. Despite that, I am positive I will return to this one many dozens of times, because it is kind of a "table of contents" for the whole field. I'll open it just for the bibliography, if nothing else.
