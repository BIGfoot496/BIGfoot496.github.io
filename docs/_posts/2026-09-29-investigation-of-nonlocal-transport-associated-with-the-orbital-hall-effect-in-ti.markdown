## A paper a day: day 19
Today's [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00359) was kind of an unintended read: it is not about the _spin_ Hall effect that I was trying to read up on, but rather _orbital_ Hall effect: charge current can induce a perpendicular flow of orbital angular momentum. The experiment design is comparatively simple: a six terminal titanium Hall bar -- a wide central channel and two thin transverse ones, yielding two Hall crosses. Along the first transverse channel they pass a current, across the second they measure voltage. Turns out the measured voltage is significantly higher than would be expected for "Ohmic bypass" -- current taking a scenic route in the bar instead of beelining straight through from one terminal to another. And as titanium has a negligibly small SHE, the authors conclude this is evidence for orbital Hall effect: charge current in first transverse wire induces orbital current in the central channel, which by inverse OHE induces a charge current in the second transverse wire.
