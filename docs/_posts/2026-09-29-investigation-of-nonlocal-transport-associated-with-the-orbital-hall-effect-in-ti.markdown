## A paper a day: day 19
Today's [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00359) was kind of an unintended read: it is not about the _spin_ Hall effect that I was trying to read up on, but rather _orbital_ Hall effect: charge current can induce a perpendicular flow of orbital angular momentum. The experiment design is comparatively simple: a titanium channel with two terminals, yielding two Hall crosses. Along the firt terminal they pass a current, along the second they measure voltage. Turns out the measured voltage is significantly higher than would be expected for "Ohmic bypass" -- current taking a scenic route in the channel instead of beelining straight through the terminal. And as titanium has a negligibly small SHE, the authors conclude this is evidence for orbital Hall effect: charge current in first terminal induces orbital current in the channel, which by inverse OHE induces a charge current in the second terminal.
