## A paper a day: day 22
Today's [paper](https://oeis.org/A122442/a122442.pdf) comes to me from Sequence Fanatics mailing list, of which I've been a member (mostly lurking) for almost six years now. It's a place of informal conversation for contributors of OEIS -- Online Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences, and one of my favourite places to browse for interesting maths. This paper is "aimed to be enjoyed by teenagers and other playful people" and sure enough I enjoyed it a lot. I never was particularly interested in Collatz, but this paper managed to cast the problem in a different light. Just rephrase it as "every number above one eventually drops to a smaller number" and suddenly there appears a very approachable universe. Still rich and vast and not fully explored, this is Collatz we're talking 'bout after all, but _approachable_!
