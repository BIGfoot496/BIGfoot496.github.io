## A paper a day: day 4
The reason today's [paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/171737a0) is what it is -- it's surprisingly short, just a tiny bit longer than a single page, and I was lazy today. Despite having spent four years in uni studying bio, I never bothered to even search for this particular historical paper before. It's a nice read, gives a bit more perspective on the history of the discovery. The fact that they actually openly acknowledged being influenced by unpublished research by Wilkins and Franklin was a tad surprising to me. The story as told in books and movies has Watson and Crick accessing that research by semi-clandestine means, but after reading the paper I wonder what actually happened...
