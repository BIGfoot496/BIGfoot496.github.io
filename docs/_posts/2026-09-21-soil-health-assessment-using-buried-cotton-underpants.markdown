## A paper a day: day 11
Today's [paper](https://doi.org/10.1002/ppp3.70259) speaks for itself. While it was as much an awareness campaign as a scientific study, it was quite successful at both. Underpants decomposition does indeed provide a reasonable proxy for soil microbial activity (at least comparable with other existing heuristics), and it is just funny enough for a bunch of people to participate. And the quote "Underwear was mainly buried in areas where population density is higher and rarely in the mountains" is just delightful out of context.
