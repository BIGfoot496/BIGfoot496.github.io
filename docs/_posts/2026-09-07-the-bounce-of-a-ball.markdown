## A paper a day: day 2
Today's [paper](https://doi.org/10.1119/1.19229) comes from the journal that I discovered only last week, but it's quickly becoming one of my favourites. "American Journal of Physics", despite the grandiose name, is actually created by and for physics *teachers*, and hence most articles are hyper readable and approachable by a much more general audience than in your average bleeding-edge journal. I've honestly just started browsing it randomly, and every article I read felt easily digestible, like sci-comm. Could really eat them like candy :-p

This particular paper pertains to the physics of bouncing balls: the author dropped them onto a piezo plate and recorded how normal force changes during the bounce. Very nice and simple experimental setup, could feasibly be reproduced in the classroom, which, I'm guessing, is the whole point of this journal.
